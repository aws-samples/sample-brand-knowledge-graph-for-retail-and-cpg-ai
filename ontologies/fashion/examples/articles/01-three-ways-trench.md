# Three ways to wear the trench this autumn

_Whimsikora Journal · editorial · 2026-09-14_

The trench coat is the one piece that survives every edit of our lookbook. Ours is cut double
breasted and knee length in a beige cotton gabardine, with wide lapels, epaulettes and a
buckled strap at each cuff. It comes with a belt, and this season we mostly leave it off.

**One.** Open over a knit turtleneck sweater dress in dark grey, fitted and just above the
knee, with a ribbed hem. Black pointed mules keep it flat and quick, gold hoop earrings add the
warmth, and a tan leather bag with a top handle, buckled straps and a chain strap does the
rest. This is the version we photographed for the season.

**Two.** Over straight-leg jeans and a plain white crew-neck t-shirt, with brown leather ankle
boots. The coat stays unbelted and hangs straight: an easy, loose silhouette that works for the
office and the train home.

**Three.** Belted tight over a fine black turtleneck sweater and a grey pleated midi skirt,
with sheer tights and tan pointed pumps.

Whatever the version, the trench's pockets are deep enough for a paperback, and the regular fit
leaves room for a sweater underneath without pulling at the shoulders.
