# Fitting-room notes: the pinstripe suit

_AnyCompany Fashion House fitting-room notes · product · 2026-08-27_

The pinstripe suit jacket is single breasted with one button and a notched lapel. Fit is
oversized through the shoulders and the chest; the sleeves are wrist-length and the jacket is
hip length, fully lined, in a navy wool with a fine chalk stripe and a flap pocket at each
hip.

The matching trousers are cut straight with a normal waist, a fly opening and belt loops; the
stripe lines up with the jacket at the side seam. They stop at the ankle, so ask for the hem to
be pinned with the shoes on rather than guessed.

Under the jacket: a cream silk blouse with an open collar and a plain front. A white pocket
square, folded flat, is the only pattern break the suit needs; if the invitation says black
tie, this is not the suit.

Shoes: flat black leather slides with one wide strap. A black leather tote with a strap of
pearls, small gold stud earrings, and nothing on the wrist.
