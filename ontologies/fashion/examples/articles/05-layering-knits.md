# Layering knits without the bulk

_AnyCompany Fashion House Journal · editorial · 2026-09-21_

The trick to layering knits is to change the weight, not the color. Start with a fine rib-knit
top in cream: a round neck, hip length, a tight fit. Over it, the cardigan: V-neck, single
breasted, oversized, in the same cream with a chunky rib and a dropped shoulder. The two are
one tone, so the eye reads one warm layer instead of two.

Below: wide-leg trousers with a high waist and a fly opening, in a cream wool crepe that falls
straight to the floor. Black flats with a rounded toe, a structured black leather bag with a
gold clasp, and layered gold chains with a square pendant finish it without trying too hard.

Two things the product page will not tell you, and the ones our buyers ask about: the gauge
(chunky, not fine) and the buttons (gold-toned metal, not horn or corozo). Both change how a
cardigan layers.
